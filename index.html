Privacy Policy for QuickSnap PDF

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Developer: Nexabytes Studio

1. Introduction

Welcome to QuickSnap PDF ("the App"), developed and maintained by Nexabytes Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your documents remain secure. This Privacy Policy details how our application handles your information, device permissions, and local document processing.

QuickSnap PDF is designed with a privacy-first, offline architecture. All document scanning, enhancement filters, cropping, PDF compression, ID card layout processing, and digital signature placements are performed directly on your local device without transferring your content to our servers.

🔒 Zero Cloud Upload Guarantee: QuickSnap PDF does not require user accounts, passwords, or logins. Your scanned documents, captured images, signatures, and exported PDF files never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share them using your device's native share menu.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in strict data minimization:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, phone number, address, or national ID credentials.
  • No Document Content Storage: We operate no external databases or cloud storage for your scans. All files reside in your app-sandboxed local storage.
  • No Account Profiling: We do not track or build user profiles.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

QuickSnap PDF requests only the minimal permissions required to provide core scanning functions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to allow you to photograph documents, receipts, and ID cards within the scanning viewfinder. The camera is active only while the scanning screen is open and you are actively taking photos. No photographs are recorded in the background.
  • Photo & Media Access: We use the standard, privacy-preserving Android Photo Picker (zero broad storage permissions) so you can import images from your gallery without giving the app broad access to your entire storage.

4. On-Device Processing & Machine Learning

QuickSnap PDF includes smart on-device processing capabilities:

  • Document Edge Detection & Perspective Correction: Corner alignment and perspective correction algorithms run entirely inside the app's local memory on your CPU/GPU.
  • Smart Auto-Naming & Text Recognition (OCR): The on-device Google ML Kit text recognition engine processes page 1 of your scan purely within the local application sandbox to suggest relevant file titles (e.g. Invoice, Receipt, Bill, Date). No text or image data is sent over the Internet for OCR processing.
  • ID Card Masking: When masking sensitive details (such as the first 8 digits of an Aadhaar card or PAN details), black privacy boxes are permanently burned into the exported bitmap's pixels. The original unmasked scan remains strictly in your private local session until you delete it.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and keep QuickSnap PDF free, we display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device data to serve advertisements in compliance with applicable laws and Google Play Developer Policies. This data may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address and general coarse location (country/city level)
  • Device make, model, operating system version, and screen metrics
  • Ad interaction statistics (impressions, clicks)

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all scanned projects and documents are stored locally on your device within the app's private sandboxed directory:

  • You can delete any page or complete document project at any time using the in-app "Delete" option, which immediately and permanently purges the files from your device.
  • Uninstalling QuickSnap PDF or clearing the app storage from Android Settings removes all locally stored scans and app preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

QuickSnap PDF is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security Measures

Your documents are protected using Android's built-in application sandboxing. No other installed application can read or modify your scanned PDFs unless you explicitly share them via Android's secure FileProvider mechanism.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Continued use of QuickSnap PDF indicates your acceptance of any revised terms.
